  • Organize a 3,200+ bookmark library with folders and tags that stay in place

Flume

Flume removes the two biggest barriers to video processing: installing editing software for individuals and maintaining a media pipeline for developers. Paste a social link or upload a file, describe the edit in plain English, and receive the finished clip through the web interface or API.

For anyone

  • Edit in the browser — trim, compress, resize, watermark, convert to GIF or audio, join clips, or download from social platforms.
  • Start immediately — 5 jobs per month without signup or 20 with an account.
  • Understand failures — plain-language explanations and suggested fixes replace raw processing-tool output.

For developers

  • Simple integration — submit a job with POST /job using a source URL and operation pipeline; retrieve results with GET /job/{id} and receive completion webhooks automatically.
  • 13 composable operations — combine trim, compress, watermark, resize, join, GIF conversion, audio extraction, thumbnails, and more in one JSON payload.
  • Scoped access control — flm_-prefixed API keys with SHA-256 hashing, secret-scanning-friendly design, presigned upload URLs, and structured error codes.

ÈgòSmart

Most Nigerians manage money across multiple bank apps with no clear picture of where it's going. ÈgòSmart is a financial mentor that lives inside WhatsApp — the app already open on your phone. Chat to link your bank account, set budgets, track spending, and get insights that actually explain your money in plain language.

  • Link any Nigerian bank account through a short WhatsApp conversation — no new app, no complex setup
  • Set budgets and get alerted before you overspend a category
  • Ask about your balance or recent transactions without opening a banking app
  • Get financial insights framed as stories, not spreadsheets
